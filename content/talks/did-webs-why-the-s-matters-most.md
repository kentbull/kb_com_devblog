At the November 2026 KERI Mini Conference I explained what the "s" in `did:webs` adds to `did:web`: a KERI event stream that lets verifiers check key rotations and domain control instead of trusting the web server. The talk also covers resolution, multisig through ConditionalProof2022, the spec's current status, and the KERI 2 work still ahead.
